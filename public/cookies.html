Cookie & Analytics Notice

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Operator: Near Cash (individual operator: Sumit Ghosh) · Contact: nearcash.info@gmail.com

Near Cash should disclose local storage, cookies and analytics in a manner that matches the actual deployed configuration.

Current Client Storage

The current application uses browser storage for selected local product state and for analytics-related identifiers where technically required. Near Cash does not intentionally use advertising trackers or behavioural advertising. Cloudflare services and Near Cash administrative analytics are currently used for infrastructure, security and operational analytics. The configuration must be re-reviewed if additional trackers or advertising technologies are added.

Purpose

Storage may support authentication and session continuity, preferences, product functionality, security and aggregated product analytics.

Third Parties

Any third-party analytics, advertising, maps, SMS or infrastructure provider must be identified in the final privacy and vendor inventory where required.

Choices

Where consent is legally required for a particular tracker or purpose, the operator must provide an appropriate choice mechanism. Dark patterns must not be used, and withdrawal of consent must not be made harder than giving it.

Changes

This notice must be updated whenever tracking technology or purposes materially change.

Risk Controls for Analytics

  • Analytics should be limited to product reliability, security and aggregate usage measurement, and must not be configured to build advertising profiles unless this notice is updated first.
  • Phone numbers, OTPs, authentication tokens, precise coordinates and message contents must not be placed in analytics event metadata.
  • Where a provider processes personal data, the provider must be recorded in the internal vendor inventory and reviewed for security, contractual and legal requirements.
  • Any new advertising, tracking, fingerprinting or cross-site profiling technology requires a fresh privacy review, and appropriate disclosures and choices, before activation.
