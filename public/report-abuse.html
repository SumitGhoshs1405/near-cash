NEAR CASH · REPORT & ABUSE POLICY

Report & Abuse Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Operator: Near Cash (individual operator: Sumit Ghosh) · Contact: nearcash.info@gmail.com

Near Cash provides reporting and blocking mechanisms to help users identify and respond to unsafe or unlawful activity.

What to Report

Users should report scams, suspicious financial activity, impersonation, threats, harassment, unsafe meetups, fake listings, privacy violations, credential requests and other policy violations.

Triage

Reports should be reviewed under a documented severity process, in the following order: immediate safety threat; suspected serious criminal or fraudulent activity; account compromise; harassment or privacy violation; and spam or lower-risk policy violation.

Possible Actions

Depending on the evidence and severity, Near Cash may warn, restrict, block, suspend or terminate an account, preserve records or escalate the matter.

Evidence Preservation

Users must not obtain evidence by hacking, unauthorised access or confrontation. Users should preserve relevant in-product identifiers and screenshots where it is safe to do so.

False or Abusive Reporting

Reports made in good faith are welcome. Deliberately fabricated reports intended to harm another user may themselves violate the rules.

Risk-Based Handling

  • Urgent safety: threats, stalking, extortion, suspected violent crime or immediate physical danger receive priority handling. Users should contact emergency services where immediate intervention is required.
  • Financial abuse: suspected scams, impersonation, fraudulent payment claims, coercion or suspicious fund-related conduct may result in temporary restrictions while the matter is reviewed.
  • Privacy abuse: doxxing, publication of private information or unauthorised location sharing may be restricted or removed where appropriate.
  • Evidence: reports should include dates, usernames, relevant messages and other lawful evidence. Users must not send passwords, OTPs or full payment credentials.
  • Non-retaliation: good-faith reporting is protected from retaliation. Deliberately false reports may themselves be subject to enforcement.
Near Cash is a technology platform for discovery and matching. It does not hold, custody, transfer, settle or process participant funds. These documents are written for the current intended India-focused, non-custodial model and must be updated if the product, entity, jurisdictions, data flows or payment model changes.
