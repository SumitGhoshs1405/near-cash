Security & Responsible Disclosure

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Operator: Near Cash (individual operator: Sumit Ghosh) · Contact: nearcash.info@gmail.com

Near Cash maintains a security reporting process and an internal incident-response procedure.

Responsible disclosure.

Please report security vulnerabilities privately. Do not publicly disclose an exploitable issue before the operator has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and mitigate it.

1. What to Report

Examples include authentication bypass, unauthorised access, data exposure, privilege escalation, injection, session compromise, insecure direct object access, or any security weakness that could materially harm users.

2. What Not to Include

Never send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, private keys, payment credentials or unnecessary personal data. Use the minimum evidence needed to reproduce the issue.

3. Safe-Harbour Expectations

Good-faith testing should avoid accessing, modifying, deleting or exfiltrating data belonging to other users, disrupting the service, socially engineering users or staff, or conducting denial-of-service activity. This page is not a promise to waive any legal rights and does not authorise any activity prohibited by law.

4. Reporting Channel

Use the official security contact: nearcash.info@gmail.com. If an in-app security or report function is available, it may be used for user-facing reports.

5. Incident Response

Confirmed incidents are handled under the internal Incident Response Plan. Where applicable, the operator will comply with CERT-In directions and other legal reporting requirements.

Additional Security Controls

  • Do not disclose secrets, authentication codes, private keys, session tokens or exploit details in a public report.
  • Security reports are triaged according to impact and exploitability. Critical issues may require temporary feature restrictions or emergency mitigation.
  • Security logs and incident records are access-controlled and retained in accordance with the internal retention schedule and applicable legal requirements.
  • Material incidents are assessed for any mandatory notification or reporting obligations before public disclosure.
  • Researchers should avoid accessing, copying or modifying user data beyond what is strictly necessary to demonstrate a vulnerability.
Near Cash is a technology platform for discovery and matching. It does not hold, custody, transfer, settle or process participant funds. These documents are written for the current intended India-focused, non-custodial model and must be updated if the product, entity, jurisdictions, data flows or payment model changes.
