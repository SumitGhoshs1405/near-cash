NEAR CASH · COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Community Guidelines

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Operator: Near Cash (individual operator: Sumit Ghosh) · Contact: nearcash.info@gmail.com

Near Cash depends on honest, respectful and lawful participation.

Be Truthful

Use accurate information and do not impersonate another person.

Respect Privacy

Do not publish another person's phone number, address, identification documents, financial credentials or other private information.

No Harassment

Threats, intimidation, sexual harassment, hate-based abuse, stalking, extortion and repeated unwanted contact are prohibited.

No Manipulation

Do not spam, create fake demand, artificially inflate reputation, manipulate ratings or operate deceptive duplicate accounts.

No Unlawful Content

Content facilitating fraud, money laundering, illegal gambling, theft, trafficking or other unlawful activity is prohibited.

Enforcement

Content or accounts may be removed or restricted where reasonably necessary to protect users, comply with law or enforce these rules.

Additional Trust Controls

  • Use only truthful, non-misleading names and availability information.
  • Do not post another person's phone number, address, live location, identity document, financial information or private conversation without lawful authority.
  • Do not manipulate ratings, create duplicate identities to inflate trust, or retaliate against users who report misconduct.
  • Do not pressure another user to move communication outside the platform for the purpose of bypassing safety or reporting controls.
  • Near Cash may restrict or remove content or accounts where necessary to protect users, comply with law or prevent abuse.
Near Cash is a technology platform for discovery and matching. It does not hold, custody, transfer, settle or process participant funds. These documents are written for the current intended India-focused, non-custodial model and must be updated if the product, entity, jurisdictions, data flows or payment model changes.
