NEAR CASH · GRIEVANCE & DISPUTE POLICY

Grievance & Dispute Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Operator: Near Cash (individual operator: Sumit Ghosh) · Contact: nearcash.info@gmail.com

Near Cash provides a structured channel for user complaints and policy concerns.

How to Complain

Please use the official support and grievance channel: nearcash.info@gmail.com. Include the relevant account identifier, the approximate date and time, the issue category and any supporting information that can safely be shared.

Acknowledgement and Review

The operator will acknowledge and review complaints within a reasonable operational period and will comply with any legally prescribed grievance timelines that apply to the service. Where the applicable IT Rules require a decision or communication within a specified period, that period will be followed. Each case should have a status, an owner, recorded actions and a closure reason.

Privacy and Safety Complaints

Urgent safety or privacy matters should be prioritised. Reports involving imminent physical danger should be directed to emergency services in parallel.

Legal Requests

Court orders, regulator requests and lawful law-enforcement requests are handled under the internal Lawful Requests Procedure.

Contact Details

Grievance Officer and contact: Sumit Ghosh — nearcash.info@gmail.com.

Business address: Kabiraj Mess, Barbaria Main Road, Barbaria, Barasat, West Bengal 700126, India.

Risk-Reduction Commitments

  • Complaints are handled according to severity, safety impact, privacy impact and legal urgency.
  • Personal information in a complaint is limited to what is reasonably needed to investigate and respond.
  • Where a complaint concerns account restriction or content removal, the operator may explain the applicable rule and the outcome, to the extent lawful and safe.
  • Where a matter requires emergency intervention, users should contact the relevant emergency service or law-enforcement authority directly. Near Cash is not an emergency-response service.
  • Nothing in this procedure removes any mandatory statutory complaint, consumer or legal remedy available to a user.
Near Cash is a technology platform for discovery and matching. It does not hold, custody, transfer, settle or process participant funds. These documents are written for the current intended India-focused, non-custodial model and must be updated if the product, entity, jurisdictions, data flows or payment model changes.
