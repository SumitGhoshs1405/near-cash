NEAR CASH · ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION

Account & Data Deletion

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Operator: Near Cash (individual operator: Sumit Ghosh) · Contact: nearcash.info@gmail.com

Users should have a clear route to request deletion, while Near Cash retains only what it is legally or operationally required to retain.

Deletion Request

Use the authenticated account deletion control where available, or email nearcash.info@gmail.com from the account email or phone context where reasonably possible.

Meaning of Deletion

Account-associated operational data should be deleted or de-identified when no longer required, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute and incident-response retention obligations.

Backups and Logs

Some information may remain temporarily in secure backups or legally required logs until the applicable retention period expires.

Safety Records

Reports or abuse records may require limited retention in order to protect users, investigate misconduct or comply with law.

Completion Record

The operator should maintain a minimal internal record showing that the request was processed, without retaining unnecessary personal data.

Risk-Reduction Rules

  • Deletion requests are verified to prevent an attacker from deleting another person's account.
  • Active safety, fraud, dispute or legal-hold records may be retained where necessary and lawful, with access restricted to authorised purposes.
  • Location data should be removed when the user disables matching or location, or requests deletion, subject to lawful retention requirements.
  • Deletion must not be used to destroy evidence after a credible safety, fraud or legal issue has been identified.
Near Cash is a technology platform for discovery and matching. It does not hold, custody, transfer, settle or process participant funds. These documents are written for the current intended India-focused, non-custodial model and must be updated if the product, entity, jurisdictions, data flows or payment model changes.
