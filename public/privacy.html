Privacy Policy

Near Cash is designed around data minimisation, transparent use of location and account data, and user control.

It is not represented as an incorporated company and has no Corporate Identification Number (CIN) or Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN). The effective date of this notice is 28 September 2026. A lawyer should verify the final notice against the deployed data flows before any material public scaling.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Near Cash may process digital personal data when you use its website, web application, account, matching, messaging, reporting, safety and support features.

2. Data We May Process

  • Account data: display name, phone number used for authentication, account identifiers and account status.
  • Matching and location data: precise device location may be collected where location permission is granted, so that the service can perform nearby matching. The service is designed to display an approximate area, rather than a precise home address, to other users. The operator states that it does not retain location history.
  • Listings and interaction data: cash-need and availability details, approximate area, match and thread identifiers, messages, confirmations, ratings, reports and blocks.
  • Security and technical data: IP and network information, timestamps, authentication and session records, security events, device and browser information where technically collected, and audit and observability records.
  • Product analytics: limited pseudonymous and operational analytics used to understand reliability and product flows.

3. Purposes of Processing

Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  • to create and secure accounts;
  • to provide nearby matching and communication;
  • to prevent abuse, fraud, harassment and unauthorised access;
  • to operate reporting, blocking, ratings and safety controls;
  • to maintain reliability, security, debugging and audit records;
  • to comply with applicable law, lawful requests and legal obligations; and
  • where required, to obtain consent for a specified processing purpose.

4. Data Minimisation and Location

Near Cash may request precise device location only where needed for nearby matching. Other users are shown an approximate area rather than a precise home address. Near Cash does not intentionally retain a historical location trail. Users shall not be required to publish a home address.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

Near Cash does not sell personal data and does not use advertising or behavioural-tracking partners. The current deployment uses Cloudflare services for infrastructure and security, and Cloudflare analytics, together with Near Cash administrative analytics. Any provider that technically processes personal data as part of the service must be documented in the internal vendor and data inventory and handled in accordance with applicable law and contractual and security requirements. Data may also be disclosed where required by law, court order, regulator, CERT-In or a law-enforcement authority.

6. Retention

Operational data should be retained only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute and incident-response requirements. The internal Data Retention Schedule is the controlling operational document.

7. User Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law and to the commencement and application of the relevant provisions, users may have rights concerning access to information about processing, correction and update, erasure, withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis of processing, grievance redressal and nomination. Requests should be made through the published privacy and grievance channel. Certain records may need to be retained where required by law, or where necessary for security, fraud prevention or legal claims.

8. Security

Near Cash uses access controls, authentication protections, rate limits, security headers, logging and operational monitoring appropriate to the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Suspected vulnerabilities should be reported through the Security page.

9. Children

Near Cash is intended only for users aged 18 years or older. The service should not knowingly create or maintain accounts for persons under 18. If the intended audience or age policy changes, the data-protection and safety design must be re-reviewed before that change is enabled.

10. Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the published notice with an updated effective date. Continued use after an applicable update may be subject to the updated terms, where legally permitted.

12. Privacy Risk Controls

  • Purpose limitation: location is used for nearby matching and related safety and security functions, and not for advertising or sale.
  • Location minimisation: Near Cash stores the current matching coordinates needed by the service and does not intentionally build a historical movement trail. Users may request location removal through the data controls.
  • Access control: exact location is not intended to be displayed to other users as a home address or an exact map point.
  • Provider transparency: Cloudflare infrastructure, security and analytics services are used in the current deployment. If SMS or OTP delivery is enabled through a provider such as Twilio, that provider must be recorded in the internal vendor inventory and this notice updated as necessary.
  • Change control: any new collection of identity, financial, biometric, contact, precise-location-history or advertising data requires a privacy review before release.
  • Deletion: users may request deletion of their account and location data. Records that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations may be retained only as permitted and necessary.
Near Cash is a technology platform for discovery and matching. It does not hold, custody, transfer, settle or process participant funds.
