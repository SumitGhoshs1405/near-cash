NEAR CASH · USER SAFETY & MEETUP POLICY

User Safety & Meetup Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Operator: Near Cash (individual operator: Sumit Ghosh) · Contact: nearcash.info@gmail.com

Safety controls form part of the Near Cash product; however, no online platform can eliminate all in-person risk.

Public Meetups

Use a busy, well-lit public place. Do not meet at a private residence or disclose a home address. Inform a trusted person of where you are going.

Protection of Credentials

Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs, passwords, recovery codes or private keys. Near Cash will never ask for your banking PIN.

Transaction PIN

Use the platform's temporary meetup or transaction PIN only for the intended confirmation flow. Never disclose an OTP or account authentication code as proof of payment.

Stop if Unsafe

If a participant pressures, threatens, follows, deceives or otherwise makes you uncomfortable, end the interaction and report or block the account.

Emergencies

In the event of immediate physical danger or suspected crime, contact local emergency services or law enforcement. Near Cash is not an emergency-response service.

Additional Meetup Controls

  • Meet only after independently confirming the other participant and the intended amount.
  • Prefer staffed public locations with CCTV or security, where lawful and appropriate, and avoid secluded locations.
  • Inform a trusted person of your planned location and expected return time.
  • Do not carry or display unnecessary personal documents, passwords, PINs or sensitive financial information.
  • Do not allow another person to pressure you into changing the amount, destination, payment method or meeting location at the last moment.
  • Never treat the Near Cash transaction or meetup PIN as proof that funds are genuine or that a payment has settled.
Near Cash is a technology platform for discovery and matching. It does not hold, custody, transfer, settle or process participant funds. These documents are written for the current intended India-focused, non-custodial model and must be updated if the product, entity, jurisdictions, data flows or payment model changes.
