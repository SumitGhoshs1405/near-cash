NEAR CASH · FINANCIAL CRIME & ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

Financial Crime & Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Operator: Near Cash (individual operator: Sumit Ghosh) · Contact: nearcash.info@gmail.com

Near Cash is intended for lawful and legitimate use only.

Zero Tolerance for Unlawful Use

Users must not use Near Cash for money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud, scams, proceeds of crime, tax evasion, sanctions evasion, theft, extortion, bribery, unlawful gambling, trafficking, or the concealment of the source, ownership or destination of funds.

No Concealment or Structuring

Users must not use matching, messaging or meetups to disguise beneficial ownership, the source or destination of funds, tax liability or criminal proceeds. Users must not coordinate transactions designed to evade legal or reporting obligations.

Fraud and Impersonation

False identities, forged documents, fake listings, phishing, credential theft and deceptive payment instructions are prohibited.

Enforcement

Near Cash may block, suspend or terminate accounts; restrict listings; preserve relevant evidence; and make disclosures where required or permitted by law.

No Financial-Service Claim

Near Cash does not provide banking, payment, money-transfer, escrow, custody or settlement services. This policy must be re-reviewed if the product changes.

Additional Prevention Controls

  • No anonymous laundering service: users may not use the service to disguise the source, ownership, destination or beneficial ownership of funds.
  • No third-party or proxy exchange: users must not arrange exchanges on behalf of unknown persons, or for compensation, where this would facilitate unlawful activity.
  • No suspicious instructions: requests involving concealment, splitting, rapid repeated matching, false explanations, fake identities or unusual pressure may be restricted or investigated.
  • Evidence preservation: Near Cash may preserve relevant account, listing, report and security records where reasonably necessary for abuse prevention, disputes or lawful requests.
  • Escalation: credible indications of serious unlawful activity may be escalated to the appropriate authorities where legally permitted or required.
Near Cash is a technology platform for discovery and matching. It does not hold, custody, transfer, settle or process participant funds. These documents are written for the current intended India-focused, non-custodial model and must be updated if the product, entity, jurisdictions, data flows or payment model changes.
