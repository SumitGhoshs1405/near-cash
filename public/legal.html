Risk and Applicability Notice

This Legal Centre is a product-specific framework. It is not a certification that every law applies in the same manner, or that every legal obligation has been satisfied. The operator must reassess the framework whenever Near Cash changes its entity, payment model, data collection, age range, geographic scope, business model, vendors or user-generated-content functionality.

The current framework takes into account the following: the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025; the Information Technology Act and the applicable intermediary rules; consumer and e-commerce requirements, where applicable; cyber-security and incident-reporting obligations; contract, criminal, intellectual-property and tax requirements; and payment and financial regulation, if the product ever moves beyond its current non-custodial model.

Near Cash is a technology platform for discovery and matching. It does not hold, custody, transfer, settle or process participant funds. These documents are written for the current intended India-focused, non-custodial model and must be updated if the product, entity, jurisdictions, data flows or payment model changes.
