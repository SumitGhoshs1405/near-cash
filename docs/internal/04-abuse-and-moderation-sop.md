# Trust, Safety & Abuse Moderation SOP

## Intake Categories

- Suspected fraud or scam
- Suspected financial crime
- Threats or physical safety
- Harassment or stalking
- Impersonation or fake account
- Privacy or doxxing
- Credential theft or phishing
- Spam or manipulation
- Unlawful content
- Security vulnerability

## Triage

- **P0:** immediate safety threat or active compromise — immediate restriction and escalation.
- **P1:** credible fraud or financial crime, or serious abuse — restrict if necessary, preserve evidence and investigate promptly.
- **P2:** harassment, privacy or spam — review and act proportionately.
- **P3:** minor policy issue — warn or educate where appropriate.

## Decision Principles

Necessity, proportionality, consistency, evidence preservation, user safety, privacy minimisation and non-retaliation.

## Enforcement

Possible actions are: warning, listing removal, messaging restriction, temporary suspension, permanent termination, block, and escalation to legal or law-enforcement authorities where required.

## Appeals

Where operationally feasible, a documented review route should be provided for account actions, except where disclosure would create a safety, fraud, legal or investigation risk.

## Risk-Reduction Requirements

Prioritise immediate physical safety, credible financial fraud, account takeover, privacy abuse and serious indicators of unlawful use. Use consistent reasons for restriction, preserve only necessary evidence, record the decision-maker, and provide an appeal or review path where appropriate. Avoid irreversible action based solely on unverified allegations, unless necessary for immediate safety.
