## Change-Control Rule

Any material change to payments, custody, location precision, identity verification, advertising, monetisation, user-generated content, international users, children, financial products or data vendors triggers a policy and legal applicability review before release.

## Risk Review Trigger

A change is "material" if it affects payments, custody, settlement, location precision or history, identity verification, minors, advertising or tracking, user-generated content, international users, automated decision-making, new vendors, or the legal entity. Material changes require a documented privacy, safety and regulatory review before release.
