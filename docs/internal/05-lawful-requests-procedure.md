# Lawful Requests & Government/Authority Procedure

## Intake

Only authorised personnel should handle requests from courts, regulators, police, CERT-In or other competent authorities.

## Verification

Confirm the requester's identity, legal authority, scope, jurisdiction, deadline and the data requested. Reject, or seek clarification of, informal requests where appropriate.

## Preservation

Place a documented legal hold where necessary. Preserve relevant records without broad or unnecessary collection.

## Disclosure

Disclose only the information reasonably required by a valid legal authority, subject to counsel review where appropriate. Record what was disclosed, to whom, under what authority and when.

## User Notice

Do not notify a user where notification is prohibited by law or would create a material investigation or safety risk. Otherwise, follow the applicable notice requirements and counsel's direction.

## Emergency

For imminent threats to life or serious physical harm, escalate immediately to the designated legal and safety lead and the relevant authority.

## Risk-Reduction Requirements

Verify the authority, scope, jurisdiction and authenticity of every request; disclose only the minimum lawful information; preserve chain-of-custody records; record the legal basis and the approving operator; and avoid voluntary disclosure beyond what is necessary and permitted.
