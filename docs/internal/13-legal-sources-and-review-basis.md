# Legal & Compliance Review Basis — India

Version: 1.0  
Effective date: 28 September 2026  
Operator: Near Cash (individual operator: Sumit Ghosh)

## Purpose

This document records the principal Indian legal materials used as the review basis for the Near Cash v16 documentation package. It is not a legal opinion and does not determine final applicability.

## Primary Official Sources

1. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).
2. Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 — MeitY; applicability and the commencement and enforcement timeline must be reviewed before launch.
3. Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, as updated by MeitY — applicability depends on the actual platform functionality and legal classification.
4. Information Technology Act, 2000 — including the provisions relevant to intermediaries and electronic records.
5. CERT-In Directions relating to information security practices, procedures, prevention, response and reporting of cyber incidents — applicability and operational requirements must be verified for the operator's circumstances.
6. Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 — applicability must be assessed based on Near Cash's commercial model and activities.
7. Indian Contract Act, 1872 and other applicable contract principles — relevant to the user terms and their enforceability.
8. Applicable criminal law, evidence law, intellectual-property law and tax law — relevant to prohibited conduct, evidence preservation, intellectual property and business operations.
9. RBI and payment-system laws and directions — a re-review is mandatory before Near Cash introduces any feature that receives, holds, routes, settles, converts or otherwise processes participant funds.

## Required Lawyer Review Questions

1. Determine which frameworks and specific provisions apply to the current Near Cash model.
2. Confirm whether, and to what extent, Near Cash qualifies as an intermediary or as an e-commerce or platform service.
3. Validate the treatment of precise GPS and location data, and of all account and authentication data.
4. Validate the privacy notice, the consent and notice mechanisms, user rights, and the retention and deletion controls against the applicable DPDP framework and commencement timeline.
5. Validate the grievance, abuse, takedown and lawful-request procedures.
6. Validate the non-custodial and payment boundary against the actual code and infrastructure.
7. Identify any licences, registrations, notices or contracts required for the current or planned commercial model.
8. Identify any product changes required before public launch.
9. Confirm the governing law, the dispute-resolution wording and their enforceability for the final operating entity.
10. Provide written redlines or a final revised policy set, and record any unresolved legal assumptions.

## No-Certification Rule

The Near Cash package must not state or imply that it is "legally compliant", "RBI approved", "regulator approved", "AML cleared" or "lawyer approved" unless the corresponding fact is actually documented by an authorised person or authority.
