# Legal & Regulatory Applicability Matrix — India

| Area | Framework | Near Cash control | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal data | DPDP Act 2023 / Rules 2025 | Privacy notice, minimisation, rights, security, retention | Counsel validation required |
| Online intermediary obligations | IT Act 2000 / IT Rules 2021, if applicable | Terms, privacy and user rules; reporting, grievance and abuse process | Applicability assessment required |
| Consumer and e-commerce | Consumer Protection Act 2019 / E-Commerce Rules 2020, if applicable | Transparent disclosures, grievance and fair practices | Scope assessment required |
| Cyber incidents | IT Act s.70B / CERT-In Directions | Incident response, logs, point of contact and reporting | Operationalise before launch |
| Contract | Indian contract principles / applicable law | Terms, user consent and change control | Counsel validation |
| Criminal misuse | Applicable criminal laws | Prohibited-use rules, abuse triage and lawful requests | Ongoing |
| Intellectual property | Copyright, Trade Marks and other IP laws | IP policy and takedown process | Ongoing |
| Tax | Applicable tax laws | Accounting and filings after incorporation or revenue | CA validation |
| Payments and financial regulation | RBI and payment laws, only if relevant activities arise | Strict non-custodial boundary | Re-review before any fund handling |

This table must not be interpreted as a complete inventory of every Indian law. It is a product-specific control map and must be refreshed for the final business model.

## Risk-Reduction Interpretation

The matrix is an applicability and control map. It is not legal advice or a complete statement of Indian law. For each applicable framework, record the exact obligation, technical control, owner, evidence, review date and counsel status. Reassess whenever the platform changes payment handling, data categories, user age, geographic scope, content functions or corporate structure.
